Your commission

Build eighteen

The club has bought 230 acres of rolling land with a creek, a lake, woods and a protected wetland. Design an 18-hole championship course. It should be fun and fair, it should be easy to walk, and it should come in under budget.

  1. Route a hole (R): click the tee, click up to two dogleg turns, then click the last point again for the green. The tee, fairway and green are shaped for you.
  2. Par follows the USGA's 2019 guidelines: up to 260 yds is a par 3, up to 490 is a par 4, up to 710 is a par 5. Most championship courses are par 70–72, with four par 3s and four par 5s.
  3. Two returning nines. Holes 1 and 10 tee off at the clubhouse, and holes 9 and 18 finish there.
  4. Shape it with brushes. Put bunkers and ponds where the landing zones are, widen fairways, clear trees, or erase to return ground to nature.
  5. Playtest (P) to watch a group play the hole. Dots show where tee shots land.
  6. Open the course to get a grade, an official-style Course Rating and Slope, and the real courses yours plays most like.

You can't build in the creek, lake or wetland, but golfers can hit over them. Anything off the property or into the clubhouse is out of bounds.
